Why It Converges
Each half is divided again into two equal pieces. The new piece is always exactly half the remaining empty space.

No matter how many times you halve it, the pieces always fit perfectly inside the original square — nothing overflows.

The infinite sum equals exactly 1 — the whole square, never more.
