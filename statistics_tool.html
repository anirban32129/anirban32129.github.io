Edit real numbers and watch mean deviation, variance, and standard deviation take shape live — the measures from NCERT Exemplar 15.

Range — largest − smallest observation.
Mean (x̄) — x̄ = Σxᵢ / n
Median (M) — middle value when sorted (avg of two middles if n is even).
Mode — most frequently occurring value.
Mean Deviation (M.D.) — M.D. = Σ|xᵢ − ref| / n
Variance (σ²) — σ² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / n
Standard Deviation (σ) — σ = √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / n]
Coefficient of Variation (C.V.) — C.V. = (σ / x̄) × 100%
Grouped Mean — x̄ = A + (Σfᵢyᵢ / N) × h, yᵢ = (xᵢ−A)/h
Grouped Variance — σ² = (h²/N²)[N·Σfᵢyᵢ² − (Σfᵢyᵢ)²]
Mode (grouped) — l + [(f₁−f₀)/(2f₁−f₀−f₂)] × h
