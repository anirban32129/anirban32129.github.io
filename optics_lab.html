Accurate point-source rays
Every point on the object emits light in all directions, not just straight across — with this on, a fan of rays at many angles is traced from the object tip, and the image is drawn at its full computed height even when the element is smaller than the object. Off, the display uses a simpler shortcut that clips the image to the element's size (dimmer/narrower framing, not the underlying optics).
