Delete Your Mathinphys Account

This page lets you permanently delete your Mathinphys account and the data tied to it. It works whether or not you're currently logged in.

Mathinphys (also published on Google Play as the Mathinphys app, and available on the web at mathintphys.com) lets you optionally create an account to submit questions through the "Ask a Question" feature. This page is the account-deletion request mechanism for that account, as required by Google Play's Data Safety policy.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently and irreversibly removes:

  • Your login credentials — your Supabase Auth account, i.e. your email address and encrypted password (or Google sign-in link), and your account ID.
  • Your submitted questions — every row you own in the "Ask a Question" data table: the question text, your display name as stored with it, timestamps, and any admin reply attached to it.

None of the interactive math and physics tools on Mathinphys require an account, and using them doesn't create any account-linked data in the first place — so if you never used "Ask a Question," there's typically nothing account-related to delete beyond the login itself.

What may be retained, and for how long

  • Active database: once deletion completes, your account and the data listed above are removed from the live Mathinphys database immediately — they are not just hidden or deactivated.
  • Backups: like most cloud database providers, our database host (Supabase) keeps encrypted infrastructure backups for a limited rolling window as part of its own disaster-recovery process, after which older backups are automatically overwritten. We do not use backups to restore or reconstruct an individual account once you've deleted it, and we do not control or extend that window beyond what our hosting plan provides.
  • Analytics and advertising: pages on Mathinphys use Google Analytics and Google AdSense, which may retain aggregated, de-identified usage data (like page views) under Google's own retention policies. This data isn't linked to your Mathinphys account and isn't something a Mathinphys account deletion can remove — see our Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Legal obligations: we don't currently retain any account data for legal, tax, or security purposes beyond the above. If that ever changes, this page will be updated to say so and for how long.
