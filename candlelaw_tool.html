🔍 The Real-World Challenge

You walk away from a candle, doubling your distance from 1 m to 2 m.
By which factor does the brightness drop? Choose the answer that matches the real law.

Why ¼?
The Inverse Square Law comes from geometry. Light radiates in all directions from the candle, spreading over a sphere of surface area 4πr². When you double your distance, the same energy spreads over 4× the area — so each part of the sphere gets only 1/4 the intensity.

Mathematically: B ∝ 1/r² → at r = 2: B ∝ 1/4. This is why distant stars appear so faint, why architects carefully place lights, and why astronomers use it to measure stellar distances!
